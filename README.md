# 🏏 The Toss Illusion: IPL Captain Psychology (2008–2026)

> Analysing 1,212 matches to reveal the cognitive biases driving the field-first obsession in T20 cricket.

![toss_win_rate_paradox](visuals/toss_02_win_rate_paradox.png)

**Key finding:** Winning the toss barely beats a coin flip (54.4%), yet captains behave as if it determines the match.

---

## 📊 5 Headline Findings

* **1. The Herd Stampede:** In 2008, 55.2% of captains chose to field first. By 2026, that reached 83.7%. The sharpest spike occurred between 2015 and 2016 (57% → 81%).
* **2. Overestimated Field-First Advantage:** Fielding first wins 57.4% vs. batting first's 48.5%. In 6 out of 19 seasons, batting first actually won more often.
* **3. Venue Blind Spots:** Eden Gardens field-first win rate is 73.5%, whereas Chepauk (MA Chidambaram) field-first win rate is only 35.7%. Yet captains apply field-first orthodoxy everywhere.
* **4. Availability Heuristic:** Dramatic close chases happen **8.2× less often** than close defenses (16 vs 131 matches), yet memorable chasing wins distort captain memory.
* **5. The CSK Contrarian:** Chennai Super Kings win 61.9% of post-toss matches while choosing to field only 59.8% of the time—far below the 73–83% rate of rival teams.

---

## 🧠 Behavioral Framework Applied

* **Availability Bias:** Overweighting memorable chasing wins over quiet bowling defenses.
* **Loss Aversion:** Fear of defending under pressure driving captains to field first.
* **Herd Mentality:** Conforming to league-wide decision trends despite venue counter-evidence.
